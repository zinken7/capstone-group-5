# Client Meeting — Question Bank

- **Project:** Autonomous Mining Equipment Market Map
- **Client:** Dr Adrian Boeing (UWA EECE)

---

## 1. Problem statement — what and why

- What problem are you solving, and what happens today without it?
- Who are the users, how many, and how technical are they?
- What does success look like by the end of semester, and how will you measure it?
- Is there an existing system this replaces or plugs into?

## 2. Client communication and MVP agreement

- What are the basic features this needs to be useful in 8 weeks?
- For those features, how polished do they need to be — rough but working, or solid enough that real users rely on them?
- What's out of scope, or where would you rather we not spend effort?

## 3. Project planning and management

- How often do you want to meet?
- How would you like us to report progress each week — a short written update, a demo, or just discuss it in the meeting?
- Are you happy for us to record this meeting?

## 4. Risk and technology assessment

- Where does the data come from, can we get a sample, and is any of it sensitive?
- Any constraints on stack, hosting, or licensing?
- Who maintains this after we hand it over?

*Note — our working idea of what the build involves:*

- *Database tables: equipment, mine site, company, evidence/verification, and how they relate*
- *Backend API: CRUD over that data*
- *Frontend pages: form submission, map display, chart display*
- *Stack (provisional): React · Node or Python · PostgreSQL + PostGIS · Leaflet or Mapbox · Chart.js*
- *Deployment to the UWA server environment*
