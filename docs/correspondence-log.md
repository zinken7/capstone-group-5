# Correspondence Log

Index of contact with the client, the representative users and the facilitator.
Maintained by M2. Add a row for every contact, in date order, on the day it
happens.

**What goes in here:** date, channel, who was involved, what was asked or
decided, and where the record lives.

**What does not go in here:** message bodies, email addresses, attachments or
exported mail files. This repository is intended to be published under an open
licence, and git history is permanent, so third-party correspondence must not
be committed. The messages themselves are held in Appendix C of the Group
Assignment 1 report, which goes to the teaching team rather than to the public.

**Meetings** are listed here for completeness; their content lives in
`docs/meeting_minutes/`.

## Conventions

- **Direction** is `Group → client`, `client → group`, or `Meeting`.
- **Participants** names people by role or member code, not by email address.
- **Outcome** records what came back, including "no response", which is itself
  evidence.
- A contact with no outcome yet is still logged on the day it is sent; the
  outcome is filled in when it arrives.
