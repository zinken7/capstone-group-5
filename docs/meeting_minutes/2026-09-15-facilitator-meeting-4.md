## 1. Testing expectations

- The main testing levels are **unit testing**, **integration testing** and **system testing**.
- Acceptance testing is not required because it would require additional client time.
- Mutation testing is optional and may be considered if time allows.
- Tests should demonstrate handling of errors, invalid inputs and incorrect user actions.

## 2. Client communication and MVP approval

- The team reported several email and Microsoft Teams attempts to contact the client, documented the lack of response as a project risk, and continued using clearly stated assumptions.
- The facilitator agreed that the team handled the situation well. However, the highest client-communication mark requires actual client confirmation; communication attempts alone cannot satisfy that level. This situation was considered in awarding 3/4 rather than 2/4.
- The facilitator will pass the concern about unavailable clients to the teaching team for future consideration.
- For the final report, the team should continue seeking written client confirmation covering the overall product/project, not only the MVP, and retain evidence of any response.

## 3. Project management feedback

- Planning was strong, especially for Sprint 1, but the report showed plans more clearly than evidence of how the project was managed in practice.
- To improve this section, the team should record planned versus actual progress, delays and blockers, workload changes, plan changes after reviews, and actions taken when problems occurred.
- One delivery was approximately one week late and affected the Project Management mark.
- The facilitator agreed that some written feedback was incomplete and should have explained deductions more clearly.
