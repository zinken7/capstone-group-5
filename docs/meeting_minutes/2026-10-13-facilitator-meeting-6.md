## 2. Product demonstration

Tyrone Tran demonstrated the staging site:

- **Map:** one marker per site, with filters for technology supplier, machine type and country. Selecting a site shows its details, and the register opens filtered to that site.
- **Equipment register:** verified records with their status, commissioning date and quantity. The table filters as you search, has a choice of rows per page, and downloads as CSV. Signed in as an administrator, **Correct** changes a record directly.
- **Analytics:** machines in service over time and new machines each year; machines by automation level, country, technology supplier, manufacturer and type, underground or surface, and commodity. Selecting part of a chart filters the others, and every chart can be shown as a table. The figures come from synthetic data, so they carry no market meaning yet.
- **Contribute:** the submission form, with the required and optional fields agreed with the client.
- **Administration:** administrator sign-in and password change; an Administrators page, shown only to root administrators, who add other administrators; and a submission history, separate from the review queue, that lists every submission and can be searched by ID or site and filtered by status.
